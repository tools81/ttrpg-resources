# Marvel Multiverse Roleplaying Game — Traits Reference

> **Player-facing reference**
>
> A Trait is a label with an associated game mechanic. Traits may represent skills, minor powers, advantages, vulnerabilities, contacts, or special circumstances. A character can gain Traits from an Origin, Occupation, or extra Trait selections.
>
> This file is an original rules summary, not a reproduction of the published text. It focuses on what a player should remember at the table. Consult the Core Rulebook, expansion books, and current FAQ/errata for authoritative wording, exact edge cases, and publication-specific additions.
>
> **Coverage warning:** The Core Rulebook contains more Traits than the free Basic Rules. This reference includes the principal Core Rulebook Traits and commonly documented expansion-related Traits, but should not be treated as a verified exhaustive catalog of every Trait in every later supplement.

## Trait basics

- Traits generally provide a specific mechanical benefit or drawback.
- Traits do not normally increase an Ability Score unless the Trait explicitly says so.
- A character receives one extra Trait for each Rank, in addition to Traits granted by Origin and Occupation.
- Some Traits are restricted to characters with particular Origins.
- A Trait can be broad and flexible; the Narrator determines whether it applies to a particular situation.
- A Trait is not the same thing as a Tag. Tags are primarily narrative; Traits have mechanics.

## Core mechanical terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Edge** | Roll an additional die and keep the better result, following the core rules. |
| **Trouble** | Roll an additional die and keep the worse result, following the core rules. |
| **Focus** | A resource used by many powers and some Traits. |
| **Ability Score** | Melee, Agility, Resilience, Vigilance, Ego, or Logic. |
| **Defense** | Usually an Ability Score plus 10, modified by applicable rules. |
| **Rank** | The character's power tier and a major limit on available Traits/Powers. |

# Core Rulebook Traits

## Abrasive

**Description:** The character is blunt, harsh, intimidating, or difficult to deal with.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on Ego checks made to intimidate someone.

**Player reminder:** This is an intimidation benefit, not a general bonus to all social checks.

---

## Anathema: [type]

**Description:** The character has a specific supernatural or biological vulnerability.

**Mechanics:** When the character touches or is exposed to the listed harmful substance or condition, the Anathema rules apply. The standard Anathema effect is a Rank-based attack/damage consequence; use the exact entry for the character's Rank and the listed Anathema type.

**Player reminder:** Record the exact substance or condition. “Anathema” is not a universal weakness until its type is specified.

---

## Audience

**Description:** The character has a group of followers, fans, viewers, or supporters.

**Mechanics:** The character can make an Ego check to gain information or resources from the audience, subject to the audience's size, interests, location, and willingness to help.

**Player reminder:** Audience is not automatic unlimited money or universal knowledge. The request must be plausible for the audience.

---

## Battle Ready

**Description:** The character is always prepared for danger.

**Mechanics:** Add **30 Focus** to the character's base Focus.

**Player reminder:** This is a flat increase to starting/base Focus, not an Edge on initiative or attacks.

---

## Beguiling

**Description:** The character is unusually attractive, charming, or captivating to people who are attracted to them.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on Ego checks against targets who are attracted to the character.

**Player reminder:** It does not affect every target and does not force a target to obey.

---

## Berserker

**Description:** The character enters a furious combat state.

**Mechanics:**

- Gain an Edge on attacks.
- Add **+2 Melee**.
- Add **+2 Resilience**.
- Add **+2 Ego**.
- Subtract **2 Agility**.
- The character cannot use ranged weapons.
- After the last enemy in the encounter is defeated or otherwise removed, lose **5 Focus**.

**Player reminder:** Berserker is powerful but changes several Abilities and imposes weapon restrictions.

---

## Big

**Description:** The character is unusually large.

**Mechanics:**

- Subtract **1** from Melee Defense.
- Subtract **1** from Agility Defense.
- Add **1** to Run Speed.
- Increase reach by **2 spaces**.

**Player reminder:** Big makes the character easier to hit in some ways while improving movement and reach.

---

## Bloodthirsty

**Description:** The character has a strong urge to kill defeated or helpless enemies.

**Mechanics:** When the character is about to kill an unconscious foe, make a Challenging Ego check. Follow the Trait’s result rules to determine whether the character can restrain the urge.

**Player reminder:** Bloodthirsty is a behavioral mechanic, not a general damage bonus.

---

## Breathe Different

**Description:** The character requires a special atmosphere or cannot comfortably breathe ordinary Earth air.

**Mechanics:** In normal Earth air, the character loses **1 Health per minute**.

**Player reminder:** This is an environmental vulnerability; it is not the same as being unable to breathe under all circumstances.

---

## Clinician

**Description:** The character is trained in medicine and patient care.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on Logic checks made for medical purposes.

**Player reminder:** The Trait helps with diagnosis, treatment, and medical knowledge; it does not automatically heal Health.

---

## Clueless

**Description:** The character is poor at noticing danger or hidden details.

**Mechanics:**

- Gain Trouble on Vigilance checks to notice hidden things.
- Enemies gain an Edge on Agility checks to sneak past the character.

**Player reminder:** Clueless affects awareness and stealth detection, not every Vigilance check.

---

## Combat Expert

**Description:** The character is especially effective against inexperienced combatants.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on Melee attacks against enemies of **Rank 1**.

**Player reminder:** The bonus is limited to Melee attacks and Rank 1 enemies.

---

## Combat Reflexes

**Description:** The character reacts unusually quickly in combat.

**Mechanics:** The character can take **one additional Reaction each turn**.

**Player reminder:** This does not automatically grant another Standard or Movement action.

---

## Connections: [group]

**Description:** The character has contacts in a particular organization, profession, or community.

**Mechanics:** The character can seek information, assistance, favors, introductions, or resources from that group when circumstances make it plausible.

**Examples:** Criminal, Espionage, Military, Police, Sources, Super Heroes or Villains, Community.

**Player reminder:** Connections are not guaranteed assistance. The contact's interests, availability, and risk matter.

---

## Dealmaker

**Description:** The character is skilled at negotiating agreements.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Ego checks made to negotiate or make a deal.

**Player reminder:** Dealmaker does not force a target to accept an unreasonable bargain.

---

## Determination

**Description:** The character refuses to give up when facing adversity.

**Mechanics:** Use the Trait’s listed Focus/Karma interaction when the character faces a significant setback. Apply the exact Core Rulebook wording for the amount and timing.

**Player reminder:** Determination is a resource/resolve mechanic, not a permanent Ability bonus.

---

## Enhanced Physique

**Description:** The character has an unusually powerful or resilient body.

**Mechanics:** Gain the specific physical benefits listed by the Trait, including its adjustments to appropriate physical capabilities. Apply the exact version associated with the character's Origin.

**Player reminder:** Do not assume Enhanced Physique grants every super-strength or super-durability benefit; those may come from Powers.

---

## Extra Occupation

**Description:** The character has experience in an additional occupation.

**Mechanics:** Gain the Traits and Tags of one additional Occupation.

**Player reminder:** This grants an additional Occupation package, not another Origin or another set of Powers.

---

## Famous

**Description:** The character is publicly recognized.

**Mechanics:** Gain the narrative benefits of fame: people may recognize the character, provide access, or react based on reputation. Apply any specific Fame-related rule used by the campaign.

**Player reminder:** Fame can be helpful and inconvenient. Recognition is not universal or automatically positive.

---

## Fearless

**Description:** The character is exceptionally resistant to fear.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate checks to resist fear or intimidation.

**Player reminder:** Fearless does not mean immunity to every mental or supernatural effect.

---

## Free Running

**Description:** The character is skilled at parkour and moving through urban environments.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Agility checks involving running, jumping, climbing, balancing, and parkour-style movement.

**Player reminder:** Use the Trait when movement is the challenge—not as a universal Agility bonus.

---

## Gearhead

**Description:** The character is highly skilled with machines and technology.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Logic checks involving machines, devices, vehicles, and technology.

**Player reminder:** Gearhead is not the same as Inventor; Gearhead improves handling and understanding, while Inventor supports creating devices.

---

## Glibness

**Description:** The character is quick-witted and verbally slippery.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Ego checks involving fast talking, deception, excuses, or verbal misdirection.

**Player reminder:** Glibness does not make lies automatically successful.

---

## Honest

**Description:** The character is notably truthful and straightforward.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Ego checks to convince someone that the character is telling the truth.

**Player reminder:** The Trait does not prevent the character from lying; it represents a reputation or manner that makes honesty convincing.

---

## Inventor

**Description:** The character can design, build, or improvise devices.

**Mechanics:** Gain the ability to invent or create technological solutions under the Trait’s rules. The Narrator determines time, materials, complexity, and whether the proposed invention is plausible.

**Player reminder:** Inventor is not a free source of unlimited equipment. The character still needs materials, time, and a suitable opportunity.

---

## Legal Eagle

**Description:** The character understands laws, legal procedures, and legal argument.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Logic checks involving law, legal research, contracts, and legal procedure.

**Player reminder:** Legal Eagle does not let the character override laws or automatically win a legal dispute.

---

## Leverage

**Description:** The character knows secrets, favors, or information that can be used to influence someone.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Ego checks when using relevant leverage against a target.

**Player reminder:** The character must actually possess useful leverage. The Trait does not create secrets from nothing.

---

## Monster

**Description:** The character is a monster or has a monstrous nature.

**Mechanics:** Apply the specific benefits and restrictions attached to the Monster Trait. The Trait commonly supports monstrous character concepts and can interact with Origin-specific rules.

**Player reminder:** Monster is not a universal damage or Health bonus.

---

## Presence

**Description:** The character commands attention and projects authority or charisma.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Ego checks made to influence, command, impress, or hold the attention of a group.

**Player reminder:** Presence is strongest when the character has an audience or social situation where commanding attention matters.

---

## Public Speaking

**Description:** The character is skilled at addressing groups.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Ego checks made to speak publicly or persuade an audience.

**Player reminder:** This is a group-speaking benefit, not a bonus to all Ego checks.

---

## Quick Learner

**Description:** The character learns new information and skills quickly.

**Mechanics:** Apply the Trait’s benefit when learning or training. Use the exact Core Rulebook wording for the time reduction or other learning benefit.

**Player reminder:** Quick Learner does not automatically grant a new Trait or Power.

---

## Scientific Expertise

**Description:** The character has advanced scientific knowledge.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Logic checks involving scientific research, analysis, and experimentation.

**Player reminder:** Scientific Expertise does not automatically provide a laboratory or equipment; Lab Access is a separate Tag.

---

## Signature Attack

**Description:** The character has a distinctive attack or combat technique.

**Mechanics:** Choose the attack type or weapon covered by the Trait. Gain the Trait’s listed Edge when making the qualifying attack.

**Player reminder:** The exact qualifying attack must be defined. Do not apply the bonus to every attack unless the chosen signature is broad enough under the rules.

---

## Situational Awareness

**Description:** The character is exceptionally alert to threats and changing circumstances.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on Initiative checks.

**Player reminder:** This is an initiative benefit, not a general Edge on all Vigilance checks.

---

## Sneaky

**Description:** The character is skilled at stealth and avoiding detection.

**Mechanics:** Gain an Edge on appropriate Agility checks made to sneak, hide, or avoid detection.

**Player reminder:** Sneaky does not make the character invisible or automatically undetectable.

---

## Tech Reliance

**Description:** The character's abilities depend on equipment, armor, cybernetics, or another technological system.

**Mechanics:** Apply the Trait’s specific consequences when the character is incapacitated or otherwise unable to maintain access to the technology. The exact rule determines whether the technology remains available and what happens to technology-dependent Powers.

**Player reminder:** Tech Reliance is a real dependency; it is not merely a description of a costume.

---

## Toughness

**Description:** The character is unusually difficult to injure.

**Mechanics:** Apply the Trait’s listed increase to survivability, such as its effect on Health or Damage Reduction, using the exact Core Rulebook wording.

**Player reminder:** Toughness is not interchangeable with Resilience or a specific defensive Power.

---

## Weird

**Description:** The character's abilities or history are strange, unusual, or difficult to explain.

**Mechanics:** No universal numerical modifier. Weird is a Trait that supports unusual character concepts and may qualify the character for certain character-building options.

**Player reminder:** Weird is not the same as the Weird Science Origin, although the two often fit together.

---

## Wisecracker

**Description:** The character uses humor, jokes, and verbal needling as a signature style.

**Mechanics:** Gain the specific benefit described by the Trait when using humor or wisecracks in appropriate social or combat situations.

**Player reminder:** The exact effect depends on the Trait’s full wording; it is not a universal Ego bonus.

---

## Wisdom

**Description:** The character has unusually strong judgment, insight, or practical wisdom.

**Mechanics:** Apply the specific benefit listed for Wisdom. The Core Rulebook errata clarifies that Wisdom is available to characters with the Special Training Origin.

**Player reminder:** Wisdom is a Trait, not a replacement for the Vigilance Ability.

---

# Additional and commonly documented Traits

The following Traits are also commonly listed in Core Rulebook references or appear in character-building material. Check the current Core Rulebook and expansion books for exact availability and wording.

## Berserker

See the Core Rulebook entry above. Berserker is especially associated with characters whose fighting style becomes more powerful but less controlled.

## Brawling

**Description:** The character is trained to fight effectively without relying on conventional weapons.

**Mechanics:** The character can use Melee Defense against attacks that would ordinarily target Agility Defense, under the Trait’s conditions.

**Player reminder:** This changes which defense is used; it does not simply add +2 to Defense.

## Combat Expert

See the Core Rulebook entry above. It is limited to Melee attacks against Rank 1 enemies.

## Extra Occupation

See the Core Rulebook entry above. It grants another Occupation's Traits and Tags.

# Traits that are easy to confuse with Tags

| Trait | What it does |
|---|---|
| Audience | Makes an Ego check to gain information/resources from an audience |
| Connections | Accesses help and information from a defined group |
| Famous | Represents public recognition and fame |
| Lab Access | Provides access to a laboratory or workshop |
| Presence | Improves appropriate group-influence checks |
| Signature Attack | Grants an Edge for a specified signature attack |
| Tech Reliance | Creates a technology dependency with specific rules |
| Toughness | Provides a direct survivability benefit |
| Weird | Has a character-building/narrative effect |
| Wisdom | Has a specific rules benefit and is available to Special Training characters |

# Character-building checklist

When selecting a Trait:

1. Check whether your Origin permits it.
2. Record the Trait exactly.
3. Write down any Ability, Defense, Health, Focus, Edge, or Trouble adjustment.
4. Note any trigger or situation that limits the benefit.
5. Check whether the Trait grants a resource, such as Focus, or changes a character-building limit.
6. Do not treat a broad narrative description as a universal numerical modifier.
7. Check the latest errata and FAQ.

# Errata and FAQ reminders

The official Core Rulebook errata clarifies, among other things:

- **Wisdom** and **Wisecracker** are available to Special Training characters.
- Other entries in the errata affect powers and are not Trait changes.

The official FAQ also clarifies that Traits from Origins and Occupations do not count against regular Trait selections, while Origin/Occupation Powers do count against regular Power selections.

# Publication coverage

The free Basic Rules contain only a subset of the Traits. The Core Rulebook contains the larger Trait catalog, and expansions can add Traits or rules that reference existing Traits.

For a genuinely exhaustive “every Trait in every book” catalog, verify each expansion against its current text and errata. This file should be used as a player aid, not as a replacement for the official books.

## Bottom line

A Trait is a rule-bearing label. When you add one to a character sheet, identify exactly what it changes:

- an Ability or Defense,
- Health or Focus,
- an Edge or Trouble,
- an action or reaction,
- a character-building restriction,
- or access to a person, resource, or capability.

If the entry does not specify one of those effects, it is probably functioning primarily as a narrative description rather than a direct numerical modifier.
