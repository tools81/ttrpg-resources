## How to read a power

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Power Set** | The themed group to which the power belongs. |
| **Prerequisite** | Rank, trait, or earlier power required before selecting it. |
| **Action** | The action type used to activate it: Standard, Movement, Reaction, or another listed type. |
| **Trigger** | The event that allows a Reaction power to be used. |
| **Duration** | How long the effect remains active. |
| **Cost** | Focus or another resource spent to use the power. |
| **Effect** | The mechanical result, summarized in original wording. |

## Core rules concepts

### Tests and defenses

Most powers call for a check against a defense or a contest. Use the character's listed ability, apply any Edge or Trouble, and compare the result to the target defense or the relevant difficulty. A Fantastic success can produce an enhanced result when the power says so.

### Power costs

Focus is commonly spent to activate powers or maintain ongoing effects. If a power has a recurring cost, pay it at the interval specified by the power; do not assume that a one-time cost and a per-round cost are interchangeable.

### Concentration

Some powers require Concentration. Follow the core rules for maintaining concentration and for what happens when concentration is interrupted.

### Power sequences

Numbered powers such as **Shield 1–4** are normally acquired in order. A character must take the earlier version before taking the next version, even when the prerequisite line does not repeat that requirement.

### Errata

Use the official errata and FAQ alongside the book. In particular, the published Core Rulebook errata changes or clarifies items including:

- **Skulk** — its Reaction trigger.
- **Dance of Death** — its Agility-check effect and Fantastic-success result.
- **Supernova** — how Focus modifies its damage.
- **Time-Out, Time-Out Bubble, and Time-Out Tag** — the cost is clarified as a recurring Focus cost per round.
- **Wisdom** and **Wisecracker** — availability to characters with Special Training.

# Core Rulebook: Powerset index

The Core Rulebook groups many powers into themed powersets. The following index is a navigation aid, not a replacement for the power descriptions.

## Basic powers

Basic powers are not tied to a specific powerset. They provide general character capabilities and upgrades.

Examples include:

- **Accuracy** — improves Agility-based damage and non-combat checks.
- **Additional Limbs** — grants advantages associated with having extra limbs.
- **Anger** — converts Focus expenditure into stronger Melee performance, with trade-offs.
- **Brawling** — allows a character to rely on Melee defense in situations normally using Agility defense.
- **Brilliance** — improves Logic-based damage and non-combat checks.
- **Combat Reflexes** — improves reaction capability.
- **Mighty** — improves physical damage and physical checks.
- **Regeneration** — restores Health under its stated conditions.
- **Special Training** — enables access to certain otherwise restricted powers.
- **Toughness** — improves survivability.
- **Wisdom** — improves Wisdom-based capabilities.
- **Wisecracker** — supports a specialized social/comedic combat style.

For the complete Basic Powers list and exact rank progression, use the Core Rulebook power chapter and the current digital compendium.

## Themed powersets

- **Elemental Control** — create, shape, and weaponize a chosen element.
- **Illusion** — create deceptive sensory experiences and misleading appearances.
- **Magic** — use supernatural effects, rituals, and magical attacks.
- **Martial Arts** — enhance close combat through technique and special maneuvers.
- **Melee Weapons** — use specialized close-combat weapons and weapon techniques.
- **Omniversal Travel** — move between realities or otherwise traverse the multiverse.
- **Plasticity** — stretch, deform, reshape, and exploit an elastic body.
- **Power Control** — amplify, alter, or interfere with other powers.
- **Ranged Weapons** — use specialized ranged attacks and weapon techniques.
- **Resize** — become larger or smaller and gain associated benefits and drawbacks.
- **Shield Bearer** — use a shield defensively and offensively.
- **Spider-Powers** — spider-themed movement, senses, attacks, and defenses.
- **Super-Speed** — move and act at extraordinary speed.
- **Super-Strength** — enhance lifting, striking, grappling, and physical endurance.
- **Tactics** — improve teamwork, positioning, and battlefield coordination.
- **Telekinesis** — move or manipulate objects and creatures with mental force.
- **Telepathy** — communicate with, sense, or influence minds.
- **Teleportation** — instantly move between locations under the power's restrictions.
- **Time** — manipulate time, including acceleration, delay, or temporal effects.
- **Weather Control** — create and direct weather phenomena.

# Player-facing power summary format

Use the following format when adding a power from a book. The wording below is deliberately a summary rather than a transcription.

## Power name

**Power Set:** Name of powerset  
**Prerequisite:** Rank, trait, or earlier power  
**Action:** Standard / Movement / Reaction  
**Trigger:** Reaction trigger, if applicable  
**Duration:** Instant / Sustained / Concentration / Permanent  
**Cost:** Focus or other resource  
**Effect:** Original paraphrase explaining what the player does, what is rolled, what target or area is affected, what happens on success, and what changes on a Fantastic success.

### Player reminder

Explain when the power is most useful, what resource it consumes, and what restriction is easiest to overlook.
