# Marvel Multiverse Roleplaying Game — Origins Reference

## Origin basics

An Origin explains where a character's abilities come from. A character may have more than one Origin when the concept supports it—for example, a hero who combines technology with extensive training.

### Traits vs. Tags

- **Trait:** A rules-facing benefit or drawback. It can modify checks, grant permissions, create weaknesses, or change how a power works.
- **Tag:** A narrative descriptor. It helps establish identity, appearance, affiliations, or story hooks but normally has no direct numerical effect.
- **Power:** A selectable ability. If an Origin grants a Power, record it on the character sheet and count it as a Power selection unless the rules say otherwise.

### Character-building reminder

When an Origin grants a Power, select that Power before spending the character's remaining Power choices. When an Origin limits Power selection, the limitation applies unless another Origin gives the character permission to choose outside that list.

# Core Rulebook Origins

## Alien

**Description:** The character is an extraterrestrial, descended from extraterrestrials, or has been altered by alien influence. The exact species and homeworld are part of the character concept.

**Mechanical package**

- **Tags:** Alien Heritage; Extreme Appearance is common when the character visibly differs from humans.
- **Traits:** Vary by the specific alien concept and any selected alien sub-origin.
- **Powers:** No universal Power package in the general Origin entry; choose Powers that fit the alien concept.
- **Restrictions:** None by default.

**Player use:** Alien Heritage is useful for establishing access to alien cultures, languages, technology, biology, and story connections. It does not automatically grant a specific defense, damage bonus, or Power.

### Alien: Kree

**Description:** A Kree-based alien character.

- **Tags:** Alien Heritage; Extreme Appearance if the character has a visibly non-human appearance.
- **Mechanical effect:** The Tags establish heritage and appearance; any actual superhuman benefits come from selected Powers, Traits, or the character's specific build.

---

## High Tech

**Description:** The character's abilities come from advanced technology beyond ordinary modern engineering—armor, cybernetics, weapons, devices, or a technological system.

**Mechanical package**

- **Tags:** Usually none universally required; the character may take appropriate appearance or equipment Tags.
- **Traits:** Depend on the technology subtype.
- **Powers:** Choose technological Powers appropriate to the concept.
- **Restrictions:** None by default.

**Player use:** High Tech is a source-of-power explanation. It does not itself mean that every Power is removable, vulnerable to hacking, or dependent on equipment; those consequences come from the selected Traits, Tags, and Power descriptions.

### High Tech: Battlesuit

**Description:** The character's abilities are supplied by a powered suit of armor.

- **Tag:** Extreme Appearance is commonly appropriate.
- **Trait:** Tech Reliance.
- **Mechanical effect of Tech Reliance:** The character's technology-dependent Powers can be treated as part of the suit/system rather than as innate abilities. The trait matters when the character is reduced to 0 Health or otherwise becomes incapacitated; follow the Core Rulebook wording for whether the technology remains available.
- **Powers:** Select suit-appropriate Powers separately.

### High Tech: Cybernetics

**Description:** The character's abilities come from implanted or replacement technology.

- **Mechanical effect:** The cybernetic parts justify technological Powers and may support Tags such as Extreme Appearance.
- **Restrictions:** No universal Power list; select the character's actual Powers separately.

### High Tech: Pym Particles

**Description:** The character uses Pym Particle technology to change size.

- **Powers:** The Origin grants access to the relevant size-changing Powers specified by the character-creation rules.
- **Mechanical effect:** The character's size-changing abilities are technological rather than mystical or biological.
- **Player reminder:** Record each granted Power and its cost; Origin-granted Powers are not free additions outside the character's Power budget.

## Magic

**Description:** The character's abilities arise from supernatural forces, mystical study, artifacts, pacts, or other magical sources.

**Mechanical package**

- **Tags:** May include Supernatural and a source-specific Tag such as Sorcerous or Cursed.
- **Traits:** Depend on the magical subtype.
- **Powers:** Choose Powers from suitable magical or other compatible sets.
- **Restrictions:** None by default.

### Magic: Demonic

**Description:** The character's power comes from demonic forces, a pact, infernal heritage, or a similar source.

- **Tags:** Cursed; Supernatural.
- **Mechanical effect:** The Tags establish the character's supernatural and potentially dangerous origin. The Cursed Tag is primarily a story hook unless a specific Trait or rule gives it a mechanical consequence.
- **Powers:** Choose suitable Powers, commonly from magical or infernal-themed sets.

### Magic: Sorcery

**Description:** The character uses learned or innate spellcraft.

- **Tags:** Sorcerous; Supernatural.
- **Mechanical effect:** These Tags identify the character as a spellcaster and establish supernatural access.
- **Powers:** Choose magical Powers separately.

---

## Monstrous

**Description:** The character is a monster or has a fundamentally monstrous nature.

- **Trait:** Monster.
- **Mechanical effect:** Monster is a mechanical Trait; consult its full description for the situations in which it applies.
- **Tags:** Often Extreme Appearance or other appearance/identity Tags.
- **Powers:** Depend on the subtype.

### Monstrous: Vampire

**Description:** The character is an undead blood-drinker.

- **Tags:** Alternate Forms: Bat/Mist/Wolf; Deceased; Imageless.
- **Traits:** Anathema: Garlic/Holy Symbols—including Holy Water—/Sunshine; Bloodthirsty; Monster; Weaknesses: Silver/Wood.
- **Powers:** Animal Communication: Bats/Rodents; Command; Healing Factor; Leech Life; Mighty 1; Sturdy 2; Telepathic Link.
- **Restrictions:** Unless the character has another Origin, the character cannot choose other Powers.
- **Player reminder:** The Origin gives a substantial fixed package, but it also imposes specific weaknesses and a narrow Power-selection limitation. Record every granted Power and every Anathema/Weakness.

### Monstrous: Werewolf

**Description:** The character transforms into a wolf or werewolf form.

- **Tags:** Alternate Form: Werewolf/Wolf; Extreme Appearance in werewolf form; Lunar Transformation.
- **Traits:** Berserker; Monster; Weakness: Silver.
- **Powers:** Evasion; Healing Factor; Heightened Senses; Mighty 1; Sturdy 2.
- **Restrictions:** Unless the character has another Origin, the character cannot choose other Powers.
- **Player reminder:** Track the transformation Tag and the silver weakness. The granted Powers are part of the Origin package and count as Power selections.

---

## Mutant

**Description:** The character is a member of Homo superior and developed abilities through the X-gene.

- **Tags:** Hounded; Krakoan; X-Gene.
- **Mechanical effect:** The Tags establish mutant heritage, possible social persecution, and possible connection to Krakoa. Their narrative meaning can change with the campaign setting.
- **Powers:** Select mutant-appropriate Powers separately.
- **Restrictions:** None by default.

**Player use:** Mutant is a strong narrative Origin for characters whose abilities are innate. It does not automatically grant a universal ability-score adjustment.

---

## Mythic

**Description:** The character's abilities come from mythic, divine, legendary, or supernatural heritage.

- **Tag:** Supernatural.
- **Mechanical effect:** Establishes a supernatural source; specific benefits come from the subtype and selected Traits/Powers.

### Mythic: Asgardian

**Description:** The character is connected to Asgardian gods or divine heritage.

- **Tags:** Supernatural; Worshipped.
- **Traits:** God Heritage; Enhanced Physique.
- **Mechanical effect:** God Heritage and Enhanced Physique provide the subtype's mechanical benefits; use the full Trait descriptions for exact checks and situations.
- **Powers:** Select additional Powers separately.

### Mythic: Olympian

**Description:** The character has Olympian divine heritage.

- **Tags:** Supernatural; Worshipped.
- **Traits:** God Heritage; Enhanced Physique.
- **Mechanical effect:** Same Trait package as the Asgardian subtype; the character's divine culture and story identity differ.

---

## Special Training

**Description:** The character's capabilities come from disciplined practice, combat training, espionage, athletics, or extraordinary expertise rather than an inherent superhuman source.

- **Trait:** Determination.
- **Mechanical effect:** Determination provides its listed benefit when the character faces adversity; consult the Trait entry for exact use.
- **Restrictions:** Unless the character has another Origin, the character cannot choose Powers that grant superhuman abilities.
- **Allowed Power sets:** Martial Arts, Melee Weapons, Ranged Weapons, Shield Bearer, and Tactics.
- **Allowed basic Powers:** Basic Powers that a normal human could reasonably possess.
- **Player reminder:** A Special Training character can be extremely capable, but the Origin does not by itself permit flight, super-strength, regeneration, or other superhuman Powers.

---

## Spirit of Vengeance

**Description:** The character is empowered by a supernatural spirit of vengeance, typically associated with a Ghost Rider-style transformation.

- **Tags:** Alternate Form: Ghost Rider; Cursed; Supernatural.
- **Powers:** Elemental Protection 2; Environmental Protection; Healing Factor; Hellfire Chains; Mighty 2; Penance Stare; Possess Vehicle; Sense Sins.
- **Recommended Power sets:** Magic: Demonic and Elemental Control: Hellfire for most remaining selections.
- **Restrictions:** The character should be at least Rank 4. Unless the character has another Origin, the character cannot choose other Powers.
- **Player reminder:** This is a fixed, high-power Origin package. Record the exact granted Power ranks, then use the Origin's restriction when selecting any additional Powers.

---

## Symbiote

**Description:** The character is bonded with a living alien symbiote.

- **Traits:** Anathema: Extreme Heat/Extreme Sonics.
- **Powers:** Environmental Protection; Mighty 1.
- **Mechanical effect:** The Anathema entries identify vulnerabilities; the granted Powers provide protection and enhanced physical capability.
- **Player reminder:** The symbiote's weaknesses are part of the character's mechanics, not merely flavor.

---

## Unknown

**Description:** The character's source of power is mysterious, forgotten, hidden, or not yet understood.

- **Tag:** Mysterious.
- **Mechanical effect:** Mysterious is primarily a narrative hook. It does not automatically grant a numerical bonus or a Power.
- **Powers:** Choose Powers separately.

---

## Weird Science

**Description:** The character's abilities came from a scientific experiment, accident, mutation, or discovery that is difficult or impossible to reproduce.

- **Tag:** Weird.
- **Mechanical effect:** Weird establishes the unusual scientific origin; it does not itself grant a universal bonus.
- **Powers:** Choose Powers that fit the accident or experiment.

### Weird Science: Gamma Mutate

**Description:** The character was altered by gamma radiation or a gamma-related scientific event.

- **Tags:** Extreme Appearance is common in gamma-mutated form; Green Door; Radioactive.
- **Traits:** Weird; Immunity: Gamma Radiation.
- **Mechanical effect:** Immunity: Gamma Radiation protects against the listed source. The other Tags establish appearance, setting connections, and radiation-related identity.
- **Powers:** Choose gamma-appropriate Powers separately.
