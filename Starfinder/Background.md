Starfinder Second Edition Backgrounds

> Scope note: This guide covers the backgrounds published in Starfinder Player Core. Starfinder 2e is compatible with Pathfinder 2e, so a GM may also permit Pathfinder backgrounds, campaign-specific backgrounds, or custom backgrounds. Those are outside this Starfinder-focused list.

Background Rules

A background describes your character's life before they became an adventurer. It supplies both a narrative starting point and several permanent 1st-level benefits.

A typical background grants:

• Two attribute boosts. One boost is normally chosen from two specified attributes, while the second is a free boost assigned to a different attribute.
• Training in one skill.
• Training in one Lore skill.
• A skill feat associated with the background's training.

If your background and class would both make you trained in the same skill at 1st level, you become trained in another skill of your choice instead. Some unusual backgrounds can modify this standard package. Archives of Nethys

Academic

You pursued formal education at a university, research institute, magical academy, or similarly demanding institution. Academics often enter an adventuring party as researchers, analysts, translators, or specialists capable of recognizing what others overlook.

Character benefits
• Choose an attribute boost specified by the background, then receive a free attribute boost.
• Become trained in an academic knowledge skill.
• Become trained in an appropriate Academia Lore specialty.
• Gain the background's knowledge-focused skill feat.

The Academic background emphasizes Recall Knowledge and intellectual problem-solving. It is especially suitable for characters who want their expertise to represent structured study rather than field experience.

Ace Pilot

You are a talented vehicle operator who might have raced atmospheric craft, flown military missions, navigated asteroid fields, or worked aboard interplanetary transports. You remain calm behind the controls even when weapons fire and debris fill the viewscreen.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in Piloting.
• Become trained in Piloting Lore or a related vehicular Lore specialty.
• Gain the background's Piloting-oriented skill feat.

An Ace Pilot is built to operate vehicles and participate in chases, dangerous maneuvers, and transportation challenges. The background does not automatically grant ownership of a starship or vehicle; it represents the training needed to use one effectively.

Bounty Hunter

You tracked fugitives, criminals, escaped prisoners, or dangerous targets for payment. Your work taught you how to follow a trail, identify a mark's habits, and bring down someone who does not want to be found.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a pursuit-related skill.
• Become trained in Bounty Hunter Lore.
• Gain the background's tracking or information-gathering skill feat.

The Bounty Hunter provides a foundation for investigations and wilderness or urban pursuit. Its benefits help you find targets, but they do not grant legal authority, a universal bounty-hunting license, or automatic access to restricted records.

Career Trooper

You served in an army, corporate security force, planetary militia, mercenary company, or another organized military unit. Drills, deployments, and strict procedures prepared you to work under pressure.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a physical or military skill.
• Become trained in Warfare Lore.
• Gain the associated combat-supporting skill feat.

A Career Trooper contributes discipline and tactical knowledge. The background represents prior service but does not by itself provide a current rank, command authority, military equipment, or continuing institutional support.

Corporate Agent

You worked for a corporation as an operative, negotiator, troubleshooter, analyst, recruiter, or representative. You understand corporate language and know that contracts, reputation, and information can be as powerful as weapons.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a social or corporate-facing skill.
• Become trained in Corporate Lore.
• Gain the background's associated social skill feat.

Corporate Agents excel when dealing with businesses, contracts, office politics, and professional networks. Any former employer, credentials, enemies, or continuing obligations should be established with the GM rather than assumed as mechanical benefits.

Cult Hunter

You learned to identify and oppose dangerous cults, secret societies, apocalyptic movements, or supernatural conspiracies. Your experience might come from professional investigation, religious service, or survival after being targeted by such a group.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a skill useful for understanding or investigating cult activity.
• Become trained in Cult Lore.
• Gain an investigation- or knowledge-oriented skill feat.

Cult Hunters are useful in campaigns involving hidden organizations, forbidden beliefs, or occult dangers. The background gives specialized knowledge, not automatic certainty that every unfamiliar religion is malicious.

Cyberborn

Technology has shaped your body or identity since early life. You might have grown up surrounded by augmentation culture, depended on cybernetics for survival, or viewed technological modification as a normal part of personal development.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a technology-related skill.
• Become trained in Cybernetics Lore or a comparable augmentation Lore.
• Gain the background's augmentation-related skill feat or special benefit.

Cyberborn characters are closely associated with augmentations and technological medicine. Any augmentation granted by the background follows its normal rules; the background does not provide unrestricted upgrades or waive later costs and prerequisites.

Deckhand

You performed the practical labor needed to keep a starship, seagoing vessel, or transport operational. You hauled cargo, repaired equipment, maintained compartments, stood watches, and learned to respond quickly when something failed.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a practical shipboard skill.
• Become trained in Sailing Lore, Starship Lore, or another appropriate vessel Lore.
• Gain the background's physical or maintenance-oriented skill feat.

Deckhands are dependable generalists suited to exploration and shipboard campaigns. The background represents crew experience rather than command authority or ownership of the vessel on which you served.

Dream Prophet

Your dreams contain visions that appear meaningful, supernatural, or prophetic. They may come from a deity, psychic sensitivity, the Drift, an extraplanar source, or something you cannot identify.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a mystical or interpretive skill.
• Become trained in a Lore specialty connected to prophecy or dreams.
• Gain the background's special divinatory or knowledge-related benefit.

A Dream Prophet gives the GM a natural way to deliver omens and symbolic information. Prophetic impressions are not unlimited foresight: their meaning can be incomplete, metaphorical, or dependent on the background's precise rules.

Entertainer

You earned a living through music, acting, comedy, dance, storytelling, stunt work, or another performance art. You know how to read an audience and hold its attention.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in Performance.
• Become trained in an entertainment-related Lore specialty.
• Gain the background's Performance skill feat.

Entertainers are strong social characters and natural public faces. Their reputation and audience size are narrative details, not automatic universal fame.

Gladiator

You fought before spectators in an arena, broadcast competition, combat circuit, or similarly dangerous exhibition. You learned that presentation can be almost as important as victory.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a skill related to physical performance or intimidation.
• Become trained in Gladiatorial Lore.
• Gain the background's associated skill feat.

Gladiators combine physical capability with showmanship. They work well as dramatic combatants, former arena slaves, professional athletes, or celebrities whose reputation was built through public violence.

Icon

You are a recognized public figure. You might be an infosphere personality, musician, athlete, activist, model, pundit, or celebrity whose name and image circulate across media platforms.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a social or performance skill.
• Become trained in a Lore specialty related to your public profession.
• Gain the background's celebrity-oriented skill feat.

An Icon is prepared to influence audiences and manage public attention. The background does not guarantee that every NPC recognizes you, agrees with you, or treats you favorably. Fame can also attract critics, stalkers, rivals, and unwanted scrutiny.

Junker

You survive by recovering, repairing, repurposing, and selling discarded technology. Where others see garbage, you see components and possibilities.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in Crafting or another technical skill.
• Become trained in Junker Lore.
• Gain the background's improvisation- or repair-oriented skill feat.

Junkers are especially effective in salvage-heavy adventures. Their training helps them understand and repurpose equipment, but it does not turn every pile of debris into valuable or fully functional gear.

Law Officer

You served as police, a station marshal, a planetary deputy, a corporate investigator, or another agent responsible for enforcing laws. You learned procedure, interrogation, and how to recognize suspicious conduct.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in an investigative or social skill.
• Become trained in Legal Lore.
• Gain the associated investigation or interpersonal skill feat.

Law Officers work well as investigators and party negotiators. Former service does not automatically grant active jurisdiction, immunity, unrestricted database access, or authority outside the organization that employed the character.

Mercenary

You sold your combat ability to governments, corporations, resistance movements, expeditionary groups, or private clients. You know how to evaluate danger and survive when a contract turns ugly.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a physical, social, or combat-support skill.
• Become trained in Mercenary Lore.
• Gain the background's associated skill feat.

Mercenaries are practical adventurers whose experience fits naturally into armed expeditions. The background does not supply a permanent employer, a mercenary unit, or free weapons beyond ordinary starting equipment.

Miner

You extracted ore, crystals, gas, magical substances, or other valuable materials from planets, asteroids, and artificial environments. You are accustomed to unstable tunnels, heavy machinery, and hazardous worksites.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a physical or technical skill.
• Become trained in Mining Lore.
• Gain the background's labor- or terrain-related skill feat.

Miners bring practical knowledge of geology, excavation, and industrial hazards. They are particularly useful in subterranean expeditions, asteroid operations, and adventures involving resource colonies.

Outlaw

You lived outside the law as a smuggler, thief, fugitive, gang member, dissident, or falsely accused wanderer. You learned to evade authorities and operate through informal networks.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in an illicit or evasive skill.
• Become trained in Underworld Lore or a comparable criminal Lore.
• Gain the associated stealth, deception, or criminal skill feat.

Outlaws are suited to infiltration and underworld interaction. The background does not force the character to remain criminal, though old warrants, rivals, and contacts can provide strong story hooks.

Priest

You served a deity, philosophy, church, temple, or spiritual community. Your work might have involved preaching, scholarship, ritual practice, charitable service, or maintaining sacred technology.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in Religion or another appropriate spiritual skill.
• Become trained in Lore associated with your faith or deity.
• Gain the background's religious skill feat.

A Priest possesses religious education but does not automatically gain spellcasting, divine powers, or formal authority over all followers of a faith. Those benefits require the appropriate class features, feats, or narrative circumstances.

Scholar

You devoted yourself to study outside or beyond ordinary institutional education. You might be an independent researcher, field scientist, archivist, historian, or obsessive specialist.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a knowledge skill.
• Become trained in a Lore specialty representing your field.
• Gain the background's Recall Knowledge-oriented skill feat.

Scholars provide expertise and investigative depth. Compared with an Academic, a Scholar can more readily represent self-directed, practical, or highly specialized learning.

Spacefarer

You spent much of your life traveling between planets and stations. Shipboard routines, strange ports, artificial gravity, and long journeys through space are familiar to you.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a skill useful aboard ships or during interstellar travel.
• Become trained in Space Lore.
• Gain the background's exploration- or travel-oriented skill feat.

Spacefarers are comfortable with the ordinary realities of galactic travel. The background gives experience with life in space, not automatic expertise in every starship system.

Street Rat

You grew up in alleys, maintenance passages, crowded station levels, or neglected urban districts. You survived through caution, quick thinking, and intimate knowledge of city life.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a skill useful for urban survival.
• Become trained in Lore connected to a specific settlement or urban underworld.
• Gain the background's stealth, thievery, or survival-related skill feat.

Street Rats excel at navigating dense settlements and recognizing danger at ground level. The background can represent poverty, abandonment, displacement, or simply an unusually independent urban childhood.

Tinker

You enjoy taking devices apart, determining how they work, and rebuilding them into something useful. Your education might be formal, but experimentation and hands-on work are your real teachers.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in Crafting.
• Become trained in Engineering Lore, Technology Lore, or another suitable technical specialty.
• Gain the background's Crafting skill feat.

Tinkers are natural repair specialists and inventors. Their feat improves particular uses of Crafting but does not eliminate formulas, tools, time, costs, or other requirements unless the rule explicitly says so.

Vidgamer

You devoted serious time to vidgames, simulations, virtual competitions, or immersive tactical environments. Your experience sharpened your reactions and taught you how to interpret complex digital systems.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in Computers or another skill associated with simulations.
• Become trained in Vidgame Lore.
• Gain the background's gaming- or technology-oriented skill feat.

Vidgamers can apply virtual experience to tactical analysis and technological challenges. This does not mean that every real-world danger behaves like a game, making overconfidence an effective character flaw.

Xenoseeker

You are driven to discover unfamiliar organisms, civilizations, languages, and ecosystems. You might be an explorer, diplomat, anthropologist, biologist, or enthusiastic traveler fascinated by alien life.

Character benefits
• Receive the background's specified attribute boost and one free attribute boost.
• Become trained in a skill used to understand creatures, cultures, or unexplored environments.
• Become trained in a xenological Lore specialty.
• Gain the background's knowledge- or communication-oriented skill feat.

Xenoseekers are effective in first-contact and exploration stories. The background supports informed curiosity, but it does not provide automatic knowledge of every species or permission to treat sapient creatures merely as research subjects.

Choosing a Background

Choose a background that supports both the character's mechanics and their place in the story.

| Character concept | Suitable backgrounds |
|---|---|
| Starship specialist | Ace Pilot, Deckhand, Spacefarer |
| Investigator | Bounty Hunter, Cult Hunter, Law Officer |
| Technical expert | Cyberborn, Junker, Tinker, Vidgamer |
| Social specialist | Corporate Agent, Entertainer, Icon, Priest |
| Combat veteran | Career Trooper, Gladiator, Mercenary |
| Explorer or researcher | Academic, Miner, Scholar, Xenoseeker |
| Underworld survivor | Outlaw, Street Rat |
| Mystical visionary | Dream Prophet, Priest |

The most useful background is not necessarily the one with the most obvious class synergy. A contrasting background can create a stronger character: a soldier with the Entertainer background, a mystic who was an Ace Pilot, or an envoy raised as a Street Rat all begin play with built-in history and tension.
