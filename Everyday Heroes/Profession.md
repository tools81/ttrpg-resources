Everyday Heroes: Core Rulebook Professions

> Scope: This reference covers the 30 professions in the Everyday Heroes Core Rulebook. Professions describe what a hero does for a living and help establish their place in modern society. They are separate from backgrounds, which represent upbringing and formative history.
>
> The precise mechanical packages are proprietary game content. To avoid reproducing the rulebook, the entries below provide descriptive rules summaries rather than verbatim text or complete numerical stat blocks. Use the Core Rulebook for exact proficiencies, wealth ratings, equipment, and feature wording.

The Core Rulebook contains 30 professions and 26 backgrounds, although some promotional descriptions combine them as more than 50 character options. Foundry VTT Evil Genius Games

How Professions Work

A profession represents the hero's current or most significant occupation. It adds practical capabilities that exist alongside the hero's archetype, class, and background.

A profession generally determines or influences:

• Ability development: Occupational experience may improve abilities relevant to the work.
• Skill proficiency: The hero gains training in professional skills or chooses from an appropriate list.
• Tool and equipment proficiency: Specialized occupations can confer familiarity with kits, tools, vehicles, weapons, or other equipment.
• Wealth: The profession helps establish the hero's normal purchasing power and standard of living.
• Professional feature: Many professions provide a situational benefit, contact, credential, resource, or form of occupational access.
• Narrative authority: A profession explains why the hero knows particular information and can plausibly enter workplaces, consult colleagues, or interact with professional institutions.

A profession is not a character class. An Athlete could be a Tough Hero, Agile Hero, or Charming Hero, for example, depending on whether the character is a bruising competitor, a technically gifted performer, or a famous sports personality.

Academic

An Academic works in higher education, scholarship, archives, or another knowledge-centered institution. Professors, lecturers, historians, archaeologists, linguists, and independent scholars all fit this profession.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes specialized knowledge, research, and intellectual credibility. Its skill choices tend to support investigation, culture, history, science, or other academic fields. The hero may also possess professional access to libraries, scholarly publications, university facilities, or knowledgeable colleagues.

Its occupational benefit is most useful when the adventure involves identifying an obscure subject, locating a credible expert, conducting formal research, or being taken seriously in an academic environment. Academic work is not necessarily lucrative, so the profession's greatest resources are usually knowledge and institutional connections, not money.

Role in the Game

The Academic is a natural lore expert and investigative consultant. This profession works especially well in mysteries, archaeological adventures, conspiracy stories, and campaigns involving ancient history or technical research.

Adventurer

An Adventurer makes a profession of travel, danger, exploration, or extreme activity. Examples include explorers, expedition leaders, wilderness guides, professional climbers, and survival personalities.

Rules Summary

The profession favors physical readiness, navigation, environmental awareness, and survival skills. Its training reflects experience operating far from ordinary infrastructure, often with outdoor gear, expedition equipment, or unusual modes of travel.

Its defining benefit supports functioning in hazardous or remote settings. The hero may be better positioned to prepare expeditions, acquire appropriate field supplies, assess environmental dangers, or draw on contacts among guides and explorers.

Role in the Game

The Adventurer is an expedition specialist who keeps the group moving when the setting becomes hostile. The profession is valuable in wilderness survival, treasure hunts, disaster scenarios, and globe-spanning action stories.

Athlete

An Athlete competes professionally or at an elite level in a sport. The profession covers team players, individual competitors, combat-sport athletes, racers, and other physical performers.

Rules Summary

Athletic work develops strength, agility, endurance, or a combination of these traits. Its proficiencies center on physical activity, disciplined training, and the demands of competition. The profession can also imply access to trainers, sporting venues, teammates, or an athletic organization.

The occupational feature reflects the hero's conditioning and sporting reputation. Depending on the situation, that experience may support intense exertion, competitive interaction, or recognition by fans and members of the sports world.

Role in the Game

The Athlete is a dependable physical-action character. The profession complements combatants, scouts, stunt-oriented heroes, and characters whose fame comes from physical excellence.

Blue-Collar Worker

A Blue-Collar Worker performs skilled or demanding practical labor. Mechanics, electricians, plumbers, builders, machinists, factory workers, and tradespeople belong in this category.

Rules Summary

This profession emphasizes practical judgment, manual competence, and familiarity with tools. Its proficiencies generally support repair work, construction, machinery, vehicles, physical labor, or occupational safety.

The profession's feature represents an ability to solve real-world mechanical or structural problems and to interact naturally with tradespeople and industrial workplaces. A blue-collar hero may be able to identify faulty workmanship, locate replacement materials, or improvise repairs with available resources.

Role in the Game

The Blue-Collar Worker is the party's hands-on fixer. The profession shines when a vehicle breaks down, a building must be entered or stabilized, machinery must be understood, or an improvised solution is needed immediately.

Celebrity

A Celebrity is widely recognized through entertainment, sports, media, or public life. Fame itself is the central professional asset.

Rules Summary

The profession focuses on social influence, performance, public recognition, and access to high-profile circles. It commonly supports persuasive or performative skills and assumes a comparatively visible lifestyle.

The hero's fame can attract attention, open doors, and influence people who recognize them. That benefit is double-edged: recognition can secure special treatment or an audience, but it can also compromise secrecy and attract fans, reporters, or enemies.

Role in the Game

The Celebrity serves as a high-visibility face character. They are effective at gaining audiences, shaping public reactions, and entering elite spaces, but covert operations may become harder when everyone knows their name.

Creative

A Creative earns a living through art, design, writing, music, film, or another expressive discipline.

Rules Summary

This profession emphasizes performance, artistic technique, interpretation, and communication. Proficiencies vary with the hero's medium and can include creative tools, performance abilities, cultural knowledge, or interpersonal skills.

Its professional feature reflects a creative reputation, portfolio, body of work, or network of collaborators. The hero may use those credentials to connect with artistic communities, evaluate creative work, or communicate an idea in a compelling way.

Role in the Game

The Creative is a flexible communicator and cultural insider. The profession suits social adventures, media-centered campaigns, mysteries involving works of art, and characters who solve problems through imagination rather than force.

Criminal

A Criminal makes or previously made a living through illegal activity. The category can include thieves, smugglers, burglars, gang members, fences, and organized-crime associates.

Rules Summary

The profession provides underworld competence, which may include stealth, deception, illicit tools, or knowledge of criminal practices. It also establishes plausible connections with people who buy, sell, transport, or conceal illegal goods.

Its defining benefit is access to the criminal world. The hero may know how to locate an illicit service, identify underworld etiquette, or approach criminal contacts without immediately appearing to be an outsider. Such contacts carry risks and obligations; they are not automatically loyal.

Role in the Game

The Criminal is an infiltrator and underworld guide. This profession is particularly useful in heists, urban investigations, espionage, and stories in which lawful channels cannot provide what the heroes need.

Dilettante

A Dilettante is a wealthy or privileged individual who pursues interests without depending on them for ordinary employment. Socialites, heirs, patrons, and affluent hobbyists fit this profession.

Rules Summary

The profession's primary strengths are wealth, social access, and broad personal experience. Its skill selection can be eclectic, reflecting hobbies, travel, education, or fashionable pursuits rather than a single disciplined career.

Its feature represents privileged access to money, social institutions, and influential acquaintances. Wealth improves purchasing flexibility but does not grant unrestricted ownership of every item or eliminate legal and logistical barriers.

Role in the Game

The Dilettante is a resource-rich generalist. The character can finance plans, enter exclusive settings, and plausibly possess unusual hobbies, making the profession ideal for globetrotting mysteries and high-society action.

Doctor

A Doctor is a trained medical professional responsible for diagnosing and treating illness or injury. Physicians, surgeons, and comparable clinicians are the clearest examples.

Rules Summary

The profession grants strong grounding in medicine, biological science, diagnosis, and clinical procedure. It also establishes familiarity with medical equipment and the institutions in which healthcare is delivered.

Professional credentials can provide access to patients, medical facilities, records, or other clinicians when circumstances and law permit. The profession improves the hero's ability to understand and address medical problems, but it does not replace any action, resource, or class feature required by the system's healing rules.

Role in the Game

The Doctor is the group's medical expert and scientific investigator. Beyond treating injuries, a doctor can interpret autopsies, recognize disease, question medical evidence, and navigate hospitals.

Emergency Services

An Emergency Services professional responds to fires, accidents, disasters, medical emergencies, and other immediate threats. Firefighters, paramedics, emergency medical technicians, and rescue personnel fit this profession.

Rules Summary

This profession develops rapid assessment, rescue skills, emergency medicine, physical courage, and crisis discipline. It also establishes familiarity with emergency equipment, dispatch systems, and dangerous scenes.

Its occupational feature supports effective action during active emergencies. The hero is accustomed to triage, chaotic environments, and working with official responders. Professional standing may provide cooperation or limited access at an emergency scene, though it is not unlimited legal authority.

Role in the Game

The Emergency Services hero is a rescuer and crisis specialist. The profession is especially effective in disaster campaigns, action scenes involving civilians, hazardous environments, and adventures where seconds matter.

Entrepreneur

An Entrepreneur creates, owns, or develops businesses. Founders, independent operators, startup leaders, and ambitious business owners are all suitable examples.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes negotiation, planning, business judgment, networking, and financial resources. Its skills support identifying opportunities and persuading other people to support a plan.

Its professional feature represents ownership, investment connections, or the ability to assemble business resources. These benefits can help arrange meetings, find a service provider, or support a legitimate project, but they do not make an enterprise infinitely wealthy or free from practical constraints.

Role in the Game

The Entrepreneur acts as a resource organizer and dealmaker. This profession can turn information into opportunities and connect the group with money, businesses, specialists, and logistical support.

Government Agent

A Government Agent serves a national, regional, or local government in an investigative, intelligence, regulatory, or operational capacity.

Rules Summary

The profession provides bureaucratic knowledge, investigative training, and official connections. Depending on the agency, it may also include familiarity with secure facilities, weapons, surveillance, or specialized equipment.

Its professional benefit comes from official status and agency resources. A valid credential can establish identity, secure cooperation, or provide access within the agent's jurisdiction and assignment. It is not blanket permission to ignore laws, and unauthorized use of agency authority can have serious consequences.

Role in the Game

The Government Agent is an official investigator and field operative. The profession naturally anchors espionage, conspiracy, counterterrorism, and law-enforcement campaigns.

Homemaker

A Homemaker manages a household and often serves as an important organizer within a family or community.

Rules Summary

The profession reflects multitasking, practical organization, interpersonal awareness, and broad everyday competence. Its proficiencies can represent budgeting, maintenance, caregiving, scheduling, neighborhood knowledge, or mediation.

Its special benefit is rooted in resourcefulness and social connection rather than formal credentials. A homemaker may know who in the community has useful information, how to stretch ordinary resources, or how to keep people functioning under pressure.

Role in the Game

The Homemaker is an understated but versatile community-connected generalist. The profession works well for heroes drawn unexpectedly into danger and for characters whose competence has been developed outside formal institutions.

Influencer

An Influencer earns attention and income through social media, streaming, online publishing, or another audience-driven platform.

Rules Summary

The profession supports digital communication, personal branding, performance, and audience engagement. Its training may combine social abilities with media production or technical familiarity.

Its feature represents an established audience and the ability to disseminate information rapidly. The hero can potentially attract attention to a subject, request information from followers, or amplify a message. Publicity is inherently difficult to control and can alert opponents as readily as allies.

Role in the Game

The Influencer is a modern information amplifier and social operator. They excel at crowdsourcing, shaping online narratives, and recognizing digital culture, but their public footprint can complicate secrecy.

Investigator

An Investigator conducts inquiries professionally. Private detectives, claims investigators, forensic consultants, and specialist fact-finders all fall within this profession.

Rules Summary

The profession focuses on observation, interviews, evidence, surveillance, and connecting clues. Its skill choices reinforce methodical casework and the ability to distinguish useful facts from distractions.

The occupational benefit provides investigative credibility, professional methods, or a network of useful contacts. Unlike a law-enforcement officer, a private investigator normally possesses no broad police powers and must rely on consent, contracts, public information, and careful legwork.

Role in the Game

The Investigator is the party's case-solving specialist. This profession is ideal for mysteries, missing-person cases, conspiracies, and any campaign structured around gathering and interpreting evidence.

Journalist

A Journalist investigates and communicates matters of public interest through print, broadcast, documentary, or digital media.

Rules Summary

Journalistic work develops interviewing, research, observation, writing, and source cultivation. The profession also provides familiarity with newsrooms, public records, media ethics, and deadlines.

Its defining benefit is a network of sources and the credibility to ask questions under the banner of reporting. Press status may provide access to public events or officials, but it does not override private property, sealed records, or lawful security restrictions.

Role in the Game

The Journalist is an information hunter and public communicator. The profession can uncover hidden stories, pressure institutions through exposure, and connect clues obtained from diverse sources.

Law Enforcement

A Law Enforcement professional serves in a police department or comparable agency. Patrol officers, detectives, sheriffs, and specialized police personnel are common examples.

Rules Summary

The profession supplies police procedure, investigation, situational awareness, and occupational equipment training. It may also establish familiarity with firearms, vehicles, restraints, communications systems, or evidence handling.

The hero's authority applies only within the limits of rank, jurisdiction, law, and current duty status. Credentials can secure cooperation and lawful access, but they do not permit arbitrary searches, arrests, or use of force.

Role in the Game

The Law Enforcement hero is an armed investigator and authority figure. The profession combines street-level knowledge with institutional resources, making it useful in crime thrillers and procedural campaigns.

Lawyer

A Lawyer is trained to interpret law, advise clients, negotiate disputes, and argue cases.

Rules Summary

The profession develops reasoning, persuasion, research, and legal knowledge. It can also grant access to professional contacts in courts, law firms, government offices, or regulated industries.

Its occupational feature concerns legal expertise and professional standing. The hero can identify legal issues, navigate procedure, represent interests, or recognize when an authority is exceeding its powers. Legal skill creates options, but it does not guarantee that officials or courts will agree.

Role in the Game

The Lawyer is a negotiator, advocate, and institutional problem-solver. The profession is useful whenever the heroes face detention, contracts, corporate conflict, regulatory barriers, or courtroom consequences.

Manager

A Manager coordinates people, schedules, budgets, and organizational goals. The profession can represent supervisors in nearly any industry.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes leadership, administration, interpersonal judgment, and coordination. Its strengths concern getting useful work from a group and understanding how organizations actually function.

The professional benefit may help organize cooperative activity, identify the person with real decision-making power, or navigate workplace hierarchies. A manager's authority is normally tied to a particular organization rather than extending to strangers.

Role in the Game

The Manager is a team facilitator and organizational navigator. The profession is strongest when a mission involves coordinating specialists, handling a bureaucracy, or maintaining order under pressure.

Military

A Military professional is serving or has served in an armed force. Enlisted personnel, officers, combat specialists, and military support staff can all use this profession.

Rules Summary

Military service provides discipline, tactical understanding, physical preparation, and familiarity with military equipment. The exact proficiencies should reflect the hero's service history and occupational specialty.

Its professional benefit represents military credentials, comrades, veterans' networks, or knowledge of military organizations. Active-duty resources remain subject to orders and authorization; prior service grants experience and connections, not unrestricted access to weapons or bases.

Role in the Game

The Military hero is a disciplined tactical specialist. The profession naturally supports combat-heavy action, survival missions, security operations, and stories involving military institutions.

Office Worker

An Office Worker performs administrative, clerical, analytical, or support work in a corporate or institutional environment.

Rules Summary

The profession focuses on administration, computers, records, schedules, and navigating workplace systems. It can also represent familiarity with a particular industry's terminology and routines.

Its benefit comes from understanding organizations from the inside. The hero may know how to locate a record, work through administrative procedures, blend into a corporate environment, or identify who actually controls information.

Role in the Game

The Office Worker is a valuable corporate insider and administrative investigator. The profession is especially effective in conspiracies, workplace thrillers, and adventures where access to data matters more than physical force.

Politician

A Politician holds or seeks public office and works through campaigns, parties, constituencies, or government institutions.

Rules Summary

The profession develops persuasion, public speaking, negotiation, and political awareness. It also grants familiarity with media relations, constituent services, donors, lobbyists, and governmental procedure.

Political status can arrange meetings, attract attention, and influence public discussion. The authority involved depends on the hero's actual office and jurisdiction; reputation and access are not the same as direct legal power.

Role in the Game

The Politician is a public face and power broker. This profession excels in campaigns involving public opinion, government action, diplomatic negotiation, or large institutions.

Professional Driver

A Professional Driver earns a living operating vehicles. The profession includes chauffeurs, couriers, racers, truck drivers, taxi drivers, and specialist transport operators.

Rules Summary

The profession provides vehicle proficiency, route knowledge, mechanical awareness, and experience under difficult driving conditions. The appropriate training depends on the vehicles central to the hero's career.

Its occupational feature supports reliable transportation and advanced control of a vehicle during stressful situations. Access to a work vehicle may be plausible, but ownership and availability still depend on the campaign and employer.

Role in the Game

The Professional Driver is the party's wheelman and transport expert. The profession becomes central during chases, escapes, surveillance, long-distance travel, and vehicle-centered action.

Religious Worker

A Religious Worker serves a faith community as clergy, a chaplain, missionary, counselor, administrator, or similar figure.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes counseling, public speaking, cultural or theological knowledge, and community leadership. It can establish contacts within a congregation, denomination, charity, or interfaith network.

Its occupational benefit derives from trust, spiritual authority, and institutional connection. The hero may obtain shelter, introductions, or community assistance where their role is recognized. This is social and organizational influence, not supernatural power unless another game feature explicitly provides such abilities.

Role in the Game

The Religious Worker is a counselor, mediator, and community anchor. The profession offers a strong foundation for moral leadership and access to communities that might distrust police, corporations, or outsiders.

Rural Worker

A Rural Worker earns a living through agriculture, ranching, forestry, or similar work outside major urban centers.

Rules Summary

The profession develops animal handling, machinery use, outdoor awareness, endurance, and practical repair skills. It can also imply familiarity with rural vehicles, firearms used as tools, or agricultural equipment where appropriate.

Its professional benefit concerns self-reliance and rural community knowledge. The hero can assess land, animals, weather, crops, and machinery more effectively than an urban outsider and may know local residents across a wide area.

Role in the Game

The Rural Worker is a practical survival-oriented generalist. This profession is valuable in isolated settings, natural disasters, creature features, and stories where the infrastructure of city life is absent.

Salesperson

A Salesperson makes a living by understanding customers and persuading them to buy a product, service, or idea.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes persuasion, rapport, negotiation, and reading social cues. It may also provide detailed knowledge of a particular market or product category.

Its occupational feature represents practiced sales technique and a network of customers, vendors, or industry contacts. The hero may quickly establish rapport or locate someone dealing in a relevant commercial good, but persuasion cannot compel an impossible or self-destructive agreement.

Role in the Game

The Salesperson is a social infiltrator and negotiator. The profession excels at talking past gatekeepers, obtaining information conversationally, and turning a cold interaction into a useful relationship.

Scientist

A Scientist conducts systematic research in a natural, physical, or applied science.

Rules Summary

The profession provides technical expertise, laboratory practice, analysis, and evidence-based reasoning. Its precise skills and tools should correspond to the hero's discipline, such as chemistry, physics, biology, or materials science.

Professional standing can provide access to scientific colleagues, publications, laboratories, or specialized equipment when circumstances permit. Scientific expertise helps the hero analyze phenomena, but it does not automatically provide an answer without adequate evidence, time, and instruments.

Role in the Game

The Scientist is the party's technical analyst and researcher. The profession is essential in science fiction, outbreak stories, technological conspiracies, and investigations involving unusual physical evidence.

Security

A Security professional protects people, property, or facilities. Guards, bodyguards, bouncers, loss-prevention personnel, and private security contractors fit this category.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes threat recognition, vigilance, restraint, access control, and protective procedure. Depending on the job, it may include training with weapons, communications gear, surveillance systems, or defensive equipment.

Its occupational feature supports evaluating security arrangements and recognizing suspicious behavior. Credentials may grant access to a protected site only when the hero is assigned or authorized there; private security personnel do not automatically possess police powers.

Role in the Game

The Security hero is a protector and threat-detection specialist. The profession works particularly well for bodyguards, defensive combatants, and heroes who plan secure entries or identify vulnerabilities.

Student

A Student is actively engaged in formal education or vocational training and has not yet settled into a long-term career.

Rules Summary

The profession emphasizes learning, adaptability, research, and developing expertise. Its skills can reflect a major field of study, extracurricular activities, a part-time job, or campus life.

Its benefit represents access to educational resources, instructors, classmates, laboratories, clubs, or campus facilities. Such access remains appropriate to the institution and the student's actual status.

Role in the Game

The Student is an adaptable novice with growth potential. The profession supports young heroes, campus mysteries, and characters who possess useful theory but limited professional authority.

Unemployed

An Unemployed hero currently lacks regular paid work. The character may be between jobs, independently supported, retired from a prior path, or excluded from conventional employment.

Rules Summary

This profession represents flexible time, improvisation, and experience outside a stable workplace. Its skill options can reflect odd jobs, self-teaching, hobbies, or the character's previous circumstances.

The profession generally has fewer institutional and financial advantages than conventional employment. Its compensating benefit is adaptability: the hero is less constrained by an employer, schedule, or occupational obligations and may possess an unconventional network.

Role in the Game

The Unemployed hero is an available and unpredictable outsider. The profession is well suited to reluctant heroes, drifters, conspiracy investigators, and characters whose lives leave them free to pursue dangers that employed people cannot.

Selecting a Profession

Choose the profession that best explains the character's adult working life, not necessarily the option that appears strongest mechanically.

Consider these questions:

What does the hero do on an ordinary weekday?
Which institution, trade, or community recognizes the hero as one of its own?
What kind of information can the hero obtain through work?
What equipment can the hero use credibly?
What responsibilities might interfere with an adventure?
How visible is the hero's professional identity?

A strong selection creates both advantages and complications. A police officer possesses legal authority but must answer to a department. A celebrity gains access but is easily recognized. A criminal knows the underworld but may owe dangerous people favors. A doctor can enter clinical spaces but remains bound by professional obligations.

|---|---|---|

Together, these layers answer different questions. A character might have a difficult childhood as their background, work as a Journalist, use the Charming Hero archetype, and pursue a socially focused class. Another might share the same profession but use the Smart Hero archetype to become a methodical investigative reporter.
