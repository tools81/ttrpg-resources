Everyday Heroes: Character Backgrounds

> Scope: This guide covers the 26 backgrounds in the Everyday Heroes Core Rulebook. It summarizes their mechanical effects and narrative roles rather than reproducing the rulebook verbatim. Backgrounds introduced by later supplements or cinematic adventures are not included.

Everyday Heroes is a modern d20 roleplaying game inspired by contemporary action movies, thrillers, and television. A background represents an important part of a hero's life before the campaign began. It is distinct from a profession, which describes what the hero does now.

The core rules contain 26 backgrounds. Foundry VTT

Background Rules

Choose one background during character creation. A background normally grants:

• An ability score increase.
• One or more skill, equipment, or language proficiencies.
• A small piece of iconic equipment.
• A situational background feature or another proficiency.

A background ability increase cannot increase the same ability score more than once. When two character options grant the same skill proficiency, replace the duplicated skill with another skill of your choice. Duplicated equipment and saving-throw proficiencies provide no additional benefit. Iconic equipment is primarily a roleplaying aid rather than a major source of wealth or combat power. Academic

An Academic has spent a significant portion of life in formal education, scholarship, or an intellectually demanding institution. The character might have attended an elite university, pursued postgraduate study, or grown up in a family that treated education as a calling.

Game role

Academics serve as researchers and subject-matter specialists. They are especially useful when the heroes must interpret technical documents, understand historical context, identify obscure facts, or navigate educational institutions.

Background-specific rules
• The background's ability increase emphasizes intellectual development.
• Its proficiencies support investigation, scholarship, or scientific knowledge.
• Its iconic equipment represents educational life, such as credentials, books, research notes, or an academic keepsake.
• Its feature helps the character locate information or work through scholarly channels. It is generally more useful for obtaining clues and expertise than for producing immediate combat advantages.

Use this background when the character's education is a defining life experience rather than merely a qualification related to their current profession.

Athlete

An Athlete competed seriously in organized sport. The character may have been a school standout, Olympic hopeful, professional competitor, extreme-sports participant, or dedicated amateur.

Game role

Athletes are physical specialists who excel when a scene involves exertion, competition, teamwork, or sporting culture. They fit action heroes who developed discipline and confidence through training rather than military or criminal experience.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reinforces the physical capability associated with the character's chosen sport.
• Its proficiency supports athletic performance and bodily control.
• Iconic equipment is typically a sporting memento, uniform item, medal, or piece of training gear.
• The feature reflects the athlete's experience with competition, training, and sports communities. It can help the hero gain recognition, locate suitable facilities, or interact with other athletes and fans.

The background does not automatically make the hero a combat expert. Its benefits concern trained physical performance and sporting experience.

Bookworm

A Bookworm learned about the world through constant reading. Unlike the Academic, the character need not possess formal credentials; their knowledge comes from curiosity, private study, libraries, manuals, novels, and obsessive reading.

Game role

Bookworms are general researchers and repositories of miscellaneous knowledge. They work well in mystery stories because they can plausibly remember unusual details or know where to begin searching.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors an intellectual or perceptive aptitude developed through study.
• Proficiencies reflect broad knowledge, research, and careful attention.
• Iconic equipment may include a favorite book, reading device, annotated notebook, or library card.
• The feature supports remembering, finding, or contextualizing written information. Its value is informational, so the player should use it proactively when the group encounters an unfamiliar name, place, object, or concept.

A Bookworm's knowledge is broad and curiosity-driven; an Academic's is usually more institutional and specialized.

Caregiver

A Caregiver spent a formative period looking after another person. This could involve raising siblings, caring for a sick relative, supporting an injured partner, working in a community, or becoming the dependable member of a household.

Game role

Caregivers act as supportive protectors. They recognize distress, maintain group cohesion, and know how to meet people's immediate needs.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports empathy, judgment, or resilience.
• Proficiencies emphasize reading people, attending to injuries, or managing difficult interpersonal situations.
• Iconic equipment is normally a personal reminder of someone the hero cared for or a practical item associated with that responsibility.
• The feature improves the hero's ability to comfort, protect, or secure cooperation from vulnerable people. It represents trust earned through patience rather than supernatural healing or mind control.

This background gives a character a strong reason to notice when others need help, even when that concern complicates the mission.

Cosmopolitan

A Cosmopolitan grew up among diverse cultures, languages, and social customs. The character may come from a major international city, a multicultural family, or an environment where crossing cultural boundaries was routine.

Game role

Cosmopolitans are cultural intermediaries. They help the team communicate, avoid social mistakes, and understand unfamiliar customs.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors adaptability and social or intellectual breadth.
• The background grants knowledge-related proficiency and may provide additional language capability.
• Iconic equipment often reflects the character's multicultural upbringing.
• Its feature helps the hero fit into varied social environments or identify culturally appropriate behavior.

If a granted skill duplicates one obtained from another source, replace that duplicated skill with another proficiency as permitted by the general background rules.

Criminal

A Criminal participated in illegal activity as a formative part of life. The character might be reformed, retired, still active, wrongly romanticized, or trying to escape former associates.

Game role

Criminals provide underworld knowledge and illicit access. They know how crimes are organized, where illegal goods circulate, and how offenders communicate.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports the cunning, nerve, or physical aptitude used during the character's criminal life.
• Proficiencies cover practical criminal activity, deception, stealth, or tools associated with unlawful entry and concealment.
• Iconic equipment represents the character's old trade without supplying a fortune in illegal gear.
• The feature establishes criminal contacts or access to the underworld. It may help locate contraband, fences, rumors, or people willing to perform illegal services.

Contacts remain NPCs with their own motives. The feature opens a door; it does not compel criminals to accept suicidal risks or provide expensive goods for free.

Daredevil

A Daredevil pursued dangerous experiences for excitement, prestige, or personal fulfillment. Stunts, extreme sports, reckless experimentation, and spectacular risks all fit this background.

Game role

Daredevils are risk-takers and action-scene catalysts. They are at home in chases, hazardous movement, stunts, and moments when hesitation is more dangerous than bold action.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reinforces agility, courage, or bodily toughness.
• Its proficiency supports dangerous physical activity or maintaining control under pressure.
• Iconic equipment is associated with a memorable stunt, protective gear, or a thrill-seeking hobby.
• The feature gives the hero an edge in a limited high-risk situation, representing familiarity with fear and calculated danger.

The feature is not blanket immunity to reckless choices. The hero still suffers normal consequences when a stunt fails.

Farmer

A Farmer was raised or worked on a farm, ranch, orchard, or similar agricultural operation. The background includes practical labor, machinery, livestock, weather, and rural community life.

Game role

Farmers are practical generalists. They are useful around animals, land, machinery, improvised repairs, and remote communities.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reflects physical work, endurance, or practical awareness.
• Proficiencies support handling agricultural equipment, understanding nature, working with animals, or performing sustained labor.
• Iconic equipment is a durable personal tool, family object, or farm-related keepsake.
• The feature represents rural hospitality and agricultural know-how, helping the hero find basic aid, shelter, tools, or cooperation in farming communities.

The Farmer differs from Rural Family in emphasis: Farmer centers on agricultural work, while Rural Family focuses more broadly on country upbringing and community.

Gamer

A Gamer developed important skills and relationships through games. This can include video games, tabletop games, simulations, strategy games, esports, or competitive hobby communities.

Game role

Gamers are systems thinkers and hobby-network specialists. They recognize patterns, absorb rules quickly, and understand gaming subcultures.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports quick analysis, coordination, or strategic thinking.
• Proficiencies reflect the character's chosen gaming habits, technical familiarity, or ability to read competitors.
• Iconic equipment might be a controller, dice set, headset, tournament prize, or customized gaming accessory.
• The feature provides a limited benefit from gaming knowledge or gamer communities, such as recognizing a simulation, understanding game logic, or contacting fellow enthusiasts.

Gaming expertise does not automatically grant proficiency with real weapons, vehicles, or dangerous tasks simply because the character simulated them.

Influencer

An Influencer became known through social media, streaming, blogging, podcasting, or another form of personal media. Their identity may be tightly connected to a brand, audience, niche, or public persona.

Game role

Influencers are public-facing social specialists. They can attract attention, spread information, and draw on followers or creator networks.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors charisma, presentation, or audience awareness.
• Proficiencies support persuasion, performance, media production, or understanding social trends.
• Iconic equipment represents content creation or the character's personal brand.
• The feature gives access to followers and online reach. Depending on the situation, this may help publicize a message, identify a fan, or obtain modest assistance from an audience.

The feature does not guarantee that every post becomes viral, that followers obey dangerous requests, or that public attention is always beneficial. Visibility can create complications.

Inheritor

An Inheritor received property, responsibility, information, or a distinctive legacy from another person. The inheritance may be valuable, burdensome, mysterious, contested, or emotionally important.

Game role

Inheritors introduce legacy-based resources and story hooks. Their past can connect the campaign to an estate, organization, object, secret, or unfinished obligation.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reflects the education, confidence, or responsibility associated with the inheritance.
• Proficiencies arise from preparation for the legacy or from managing it.
• Iconic equipment is often part of the inheritance itself, but it remains within the background's modest equipment limits unless the GM makes it a campaign element.
• The feature grants limited access to inherited resources, information, or recognition.

An inheritance does not bypass the game's Wealth system merely because the narrative involves a rich relative. Major assets should remain under GM control and serve the campaign rather than function as unrestricted starting equipment.

Larper

A Larper participates in live-action roleplaying. The character has experience with costumes, fictional personas, improvised performance, simulated combat, event organization, and immersive communities.

Game role

Larpers are creative improvisers. They are suited to disguise, performance, prop recognition, social roleplaying, and unconventional planning.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports physical coordination, creativity, or social performance.
• Proficiencies represent acting, costuming, crafting, or familiarity with simulated weapons.
• Iconic equipment is a costume piece, foam weapon, character token, or event keepsake.
• The feature draws on larp communities and practiced improvisation, helping the hero acquire hobby resources, identify participants, or convincingly inhabit an assumed role.

Training with safe simulated weapons does not automatically equal real combat training unless the background explicitly grants the relevant equipment proficiency.

Latchkey Kid

A Latchkey Kid spent a formative period handling daily life with limited adult supervision. They learned to solve problems independently, navigate neighborhoods, manage small emergencies, and avoid drawing unwanted attention.

Game role

Latchkey Kids are self-reliant survivors. They are good at finding practical solutions, entering overlooked spaces, and reading the rhythms of homes and neighborhoods.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors adaptability, caution, or personal resourcefulness.
• Proficiencies support observation, stealth, everyday problem-solving, or urban navigation.
• Iconic equipment is something connected to childhood independence, commonly a key, improvised tool, or cherished household object.
• The feature represents knowing how to look after oneself with minimal resources or supervision.

The background does not imply neglect in every case. It can represent a loving household in which circumstances required unusual independence.

Local Hero

A Local Hero is admired for something done on behalf of a community. The deed might involve a rescue, public service, championship, disaster response, or standing up to a local threat.

Game role

Local Heroes are community-facing leaders. They can secure trust, cooperation, and recognition where their story is known.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports confidence, courage, or dependability.
• Proficiencies arise from the deed that earned the character's reputation.
• Iconic equipment is usually an award, article, photograph, or object linked to that achievement.
• The feature grants recognition and goodwill within an appropriate community. Locals may provide information, modest assistance, or a safe place to regroup.

Recognition has a limited geographic or social reach. Being a hero in one town does not make the character universally famous.

Military Brat

A Military Brat grew up in a military family or around military installations. Frequent moves, formal expectations, military terminology, and exposure to service culture shaped the character.

Game role

Military Brats are adaptable cultural insiders. They understand military communities without necessarily being trained soldiers themselves.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reflects discipline, adaptability, or social awareness.
• Proficiencies cover military culture, travel, vehicles, or skills learned through repeated relocation.
• Iconic equipment might be a unit patch, challenge coin, photograph, or object from a former posting.
• The feature helps the hero navigate military communities and recognize service customs, potentially locating sympathetic contacts or understanding installation life.

This background does not grant rank, security clearance, or command authority. It represents familiarity, not active service.

Misfit

A Misfit never fit comfortably into the expected social mold. The character may have belonged to an unusual subculture, rejected conventional expectations, or learned to observe society from its margins.

Game role

Misfits are outsider specialists. They notice assumptions others overlook and can connect with people who distrust mainstream institutions.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports individuality, perception, or force of personality.
• Proficiencies reflect habits developed outside ordinary social structures.
• Iconic equipment symbolizes the character's identity or affiliation.
• The feature helps the hero find acceptance among outsiders or obtain unconventional information.

The background should not be treated as a diagnosis or as permission to portray marginalized people as inherently unstable. Its game function is social independence and outsider perspective.

Motorhead

A Motorhead is devoted to cars, motorcycles, engines, and vehicle culture. The character may have grown up in a garage, raced informally, restored classic vehicles, or spent every spare hour modifying machines.

Game role

Motorheads are vehicle and mechanical specialists. They shine in campaigns featuring road travel, chases, breakdowns, garages, and automotive subcultures.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports coordination, practical intelligence, or mechanical aptitude.
• Proficiencies emphasize Vehicles, repairs, or related technical work.
• Iconic equipment is usually a favored tool, vehicle part, racing souvenir, or garage keepsake.
• The feature grants help through vehicle culture and garage networks, such as identifying a modification, finding a workspace, or locating someone able to perform specialized automotive work.

The feature does not provide free vehicles or eliminate the time, parts, and Wealth normally required for major repairs.

On the Run

Someone On the Run is hiding from a person, organization, government, criminal group, or dangerous past. Whether guilty, innocent, or morally complicated, the character has learned to avoid being found.

Game role

This background produces an evasive survivor. The hero understands concealment, temporary identities, surveillance risks, and the practical demands of disappearing.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors alertness, nerve, or adaptability.
• Proficiencies support deception, stealth, observation, or survival while moving between safe locations.
• Iconic equipment may include a false identity, hidden emergency item, old photograph, or object connected to the pursuit.
• The feature helps the character conceal their trail or find short-term refuge, subject to the circumstances and the pursuer's reach.

The feature does not make the hero untraceable. Using public systems, contacting known associates, or attracting attention can still expose the character.

Raised by Assassins

A hero Raised by Assassins grew up among professional killers, covert enforcers, or a secretive organization that normalized lethal methods. The character might embrace, reject, or flee that upbringing.

Game role

This background creates a covert-action survivor with personal familiarity with disciplined violence. It works well for characters whose competence comes with dangerous family ties.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports precision, agility, awareness, or emotional control.
• Proficiencies reflect stealth, covert movement, observation, or weapon familiarity.
• Iconic equipment is a training relic, coded token, concealed tool, or reminder of the organization.
• The feature grants insight into assassins and their methods, potentially helping identify professional techniques, recognize warning signs, or contact someone from that world.

The background does not grant automatic kills, immunity to surprise, or unrestricted access to illegal weapons. Former mentors and organizations may also be liabilities.

Raised by Spies

A hero Raised by Spies learned espionage culture at home. Coded language, cover stories, secrecy, international movement, and careful observation were part of ordinary life.

Game role

This background supports an espionage-oriented generalist. The character can recognize suspicious behavior, improvise cover identities, and understand the logic of intelligence work.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors social adaptability, intellect, or perceptiveness.
• Proficiencies support deception, investigation, languages, or covert tradecraft.
• Iconic equipment might be a codebook, dead-drop token, false document, or disguised keepsake.
• The feature provides familiarity with spycraft and intelligence contacts, helping identify espionage techniques or establish discreet communication.

Unlike a professional operative, the character may lack formal agency authority, resources, and clearance. Their expertise comes from upbringing.

Rural Family

A hero from a Rural Family grew up in a small town, remote settlement, or close-knit country community. Family ties, practical cooperation, and familiarity with the land shaped their outlook.

Game role

The background creates a grounded survivor with community ties. It is useful in wilderness travel, small-town investigations, and stories where reputation and family networks matter.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors toughness, awareness, or practical judgment.
• Proficiencies reflect outdoor life, local travel, animals, tools, or small-community interaction.
• Iconic equipment is generally a family heirloom or durable object from home.
• The feature provides family or community support in rural areas, such as modest hospitality, introductions, or practical local information.

Support is based on relationships and reciprocity. It is not unlimited, and reckless behavior can damage the family's reputation.

Scion

A Scion comes from a powerful, wealthy, famous, or influential family. Unlike the Inheritor, whose defining element is something received, the Scion's defining element is continuing membership in a living family network.

Game role

Scions are privileged social connectors. They gain access to places, people, and institutions that may be closed to ordinary heroes.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reflects confidence, education, or social polish.
• Proficiencies support elite social settings, leadership, or knowledge expected of the family.
• Iconic equipment displays family identity without functioning as unlimited wealth.
• The feature provides access through the family's name and influence, potentially securing an introduction, audience, or consideration from powerful people.

Influence is not control. Family members can impose expectations, withhold help, or demand favors. The feature also does not replace the profession-based Wealth rules.

Self-Taught

A Self-Taught hero mastered a field without conventional schooling. The character learned through experimentation, observation, manuals, online resources, mentors, and repeated failure.

Game role

Self-Taught heroes are flexible specialists and unconventional problem-solvers. Their knowledge may be narrow but deep, and they often approach problems differently from formally trained experts.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase reflects the aptitude the character cultivated independently.
• The background's proficiencies are selected or framed around the chosen area of self-directed study.
• Iconic equipment is a homemade tool, heavily used manual, prototype, or artifact of early experiments.
• The feature supports independent learning or practical improvisation, especially when formal credentials are less important than results.

Self-teaching does not automatically grant licenses, institutional access, or legal authority. The hero may know the work while lacking documentation that proves it.

Street Kid

A Street Kid grew up navigating urban public spaces with limited protection or stability. They learned which neighborhoods were safe, how to read strangers, and where to find necessities.

Game role

Street Kids are urban survival experts. They find routes, shelter, rumors, and people who operate below official notice.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase favors agility, awareness, or resilience.
• Proficiencies emphasize stealth, streetwise interaction, observation, or movement through cities.
• Iconic equipment is a keepsake or practical object from the character's early life.
• The feature gives access to street networks and urban survival knowledge, helping locate temporary shelter, local rumors, or someone familiar with a neighborhood.

Street contacts provide modest help according to their means. They are not a private intelligence service and should not be treated as disposable resources.

Troublemaker

A Troublemaker developed a reputation for breaking rules, starting fights, pulling dangerous pranks, provoking authorities, or challenging anyone who tried to control them.

Game role

Troublemakers are disruptors. They draw attention, unsettle opponents, and understand how authority figures react when challenged.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports boldness, toughness, agility, or force of personality.
• Proficiencies reflect intimidation, deception, stealth, physical confrontation, or evading consequences.
• Iconic equipment is a trophy from a notorious incident, disciplinary record, or symbol of rebellion.
• The feature helps the hero connect with fellow rebels or exploit a reputation for defiance.

The feature does not erase legal consequences. Creating a distraction can help the team, but it may also make the Troublemaker the obvious suspect.

World Traveler

A World Traveler has spent substantial time crossing national and cultural boundaries. Travel may have come through family, work, personal wealth, backpacking, aid work, or a restless desire to explore.

Game role

World Travelers are global navigators. They are valuable in international campaigns because they understand travel logistics, cultural adjustment, and the realities of arriving somewhere unfamiliar.

Background-specific rules
• The ability increase supports adaptability, perception, or social confidence.
• The background grants travel-related proficiency and commonly emphasizes languages or cultural familiarity.
• Iconic equipment is a passport, souvenir, map, travel journal, or object associated with a memorable journey.
• The feature helps the hero find contacts, accommodations, directions, or basic support while abroad.

The feature smooths ordinary travel problems but does not override borders, visas, law enforcement, hostile governments, or the Wealth required for expensive transportation.

Choosing a Background

Choose a background based first on the formative experience you want to explore. The mechanical benefits should support that history, not replace it.

Three distinctions are particularly useful:

• Background versus profession: A Criminal background can now be a police officer, while an Academic background can currently work as a bartender. Background is then; profession is now.
• Feature versus automatic success: A feature grants a specific opportunity, contact, or situational advantage. It does not force NPCs to cooperate or remove the need for checks when the outcome is uncertain.
• Narrative resource versus wealth: Family connections, inheritances, followers, and contacts do not provide unlimited money or equipment. The game's Wealth system and the GM still determine what is available.

Creating a Custom Background

With the GM's approval, a custom background can be built using the core framework:

Grant +1 to one ability score.
Grant one skill or equipment proficiency.
Provide a piece of iconic equipment that is evocative but not exceptionally valuable.
Add either a second proficiency or language, or a useful feature that is limited by circumstance or frequency.

The feature should open a plausible opportunity rather than guarantee success. Good examples include locating a specialized community, securing modest hospitality, recognizing a distinctive practice, or obtaining an introduction to an appropriate contact. Everyday Heroes SRD
