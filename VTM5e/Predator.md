# Vampire: The Masquerade - Predator Options

In Vampire: The Masquerade 5th edition, players can choose from a variety of predator types, each with unique mechanics and traits. Below is a comprehensive overview of these different hunting styles.

## Alleycat
Those who find violence to be the quickest way to get what they want might gravitate towards this hunting style. Alleycats feed by brute force and intimidation, making their victims cower or even using Dominance to mask their actions.

- **Dice Pools**: 
  - Strength + Brawl for forceful feeding
  - Wits + Streetwise to find criminals

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Intimidation (Stickups) or Brawl (Grappling)
  - One dot of either Celerity or Potence
  - Three dots of Criminal Contacts

- **Losses**:
  - One dot of Humanity

## Bagger
Baggers consume preserved or rancid blood, allowing them options others do not. They might work in medical settings or have connections to the black market for blood.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Intelligence + Streetwise to procure blood supplies

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Larceny (Lock Picking) or Streetwise (Black Market)
  - One dot of Blood Sorcery (Tremere and Banu Haqim only), Oblivion (Hecata only), or Obfuscate
  - The Feeding Merit (•••) Iron Gullet
  - An Enemy Flaw (••)

## Blood Leech
Blood Leeches prefer to feed on other vampires, viewing the consumption of mortal blood as distasteful.

- **Dice Pool**: Not abstracted down to a dice pool

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Brawl (Kindred) or Stealth (Against Kindred)
  - One dot of Celerity or Protean
  - Increased blood potency by one
  - Dark Secret Flaw or Shunned Flaw
  - Feeding Flaw: (••) Prey Exclusion (Mortals)

## Cleaver
Cleavers feed on friends and family, maintaining close relationships while secretly feeding on them. This practice can be risky and often results in secrecy.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Manipulation + Subterfuge to condition victims

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Persuasion (Gaslighting) or Subterfuge (Coverups)
  - One dot of Dominate or Animalism
  - Dark Secret Flaw (•) Cleaver
  - Herd Advantage (••)

## Consensualist
Consensualists obtain blood only with the victim's permission, often using various methods to gain consent.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Manipulation + Persuasion for consensual feeding

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Medicine (Phlebotomy) or Persuasion (Vessels)
  - One dot of Auspex or Fortitude
  - One dot of Humanity
  - Dark Secret Flaw: (•) Masquerade Breacher
  - Feeding Flaw: (•) Prey Exclusion (Non-consenting)

## Farmer
Farmers restrict their feeding to animals, avoiding mortal kill whenever possible.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Composure + Animal Ken to find and catch animals

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Animal Ken (specific animal) or Survival (Hunting)
  - One dot of Animalism or Protean
  - One dot of Humanity
  - Feeding Flaw: (••) Farmer

## Osiris
Osiris frequently feed from adoring fans or followers, using their charisma to gather blood.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Manipulation + Subterfuge or Intimidation + Fame to feed from followers

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Occult (specific tradition) or Performance (specific entertainment field)
  - One dot of Blood Sorcery (Tremere or Banu Haqim only) or Presence
  - Spend three dots between fame and herd backgrounds
  - Spend two dots on enemies or mythic flaws

## Sandman
Sandmen prefer to feed on sleeping mortals, making it less likely for them to be caught.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Dexterity + Stealth for casing locations

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Medicine (Anesthetics) or Stealth (Break-in)
  - One dot of Auspex or Obfuscate
  - One dot of Resources

## Scene Queen
Scene Queens navigate their particular subcultures and thrive by feeding off those who adore them.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Manipulation + Persuasion to extract blood from admirers

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Etiquette (specific scene), Leadership (specific scene), or Streetwise (specific scene)
  - One dot of Dominate or Potence
  - Fame Advantage (•)
  - Contact Advantage (•)

## Siren
Sirens use sexual appeal to seduce their victims, often blurring the lines of consent.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Charisma + Subterfuge to manipulate potential victims

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Persuasion (Seduction) or Subterfuge (Seduction)
  - One dot of Fortitude or Presence
  - Looks Merit: (••) Beautiful
  - Enemy Flaw (•) A spurned lover or jealous partner

## Extortionist
Extortionists leverage their services for blood, often relying on fabricated threats to coerce victims.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Strength/Manipulation + Intimidation to extract blood

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Intimidation (Coercion) or Larceny (Security)
  - One dot of Dominate or Potence
  - Spend three dots between Contacts and Resources backgrounds
  - Enemy Flaw (••)

## Graverobber
Graverobbers seek blood from corpses or mourners, often finding themselves in morbid situations.

- **Dice Pools**: 
  - Resolve + Medicine for locating suitable bodies
  - Manipulation + Insight for navigating mourners

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Occult (Grave Rituals) or Medicine (Cadavers)
  - One dot of Fortitude or Oblivion
  - The Feeding Merit (•••) Iron Gullet
  - Haven Advantage (•)
  - Herd Flaw: (••) Obvious Predator

## Roadside Killer
These Kindred are mobile hunters who target those who are easily discarded.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Dexterity/Charisma + Drive to pick up victims

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Survival (the road) or Investigation (vampire cant)
  - One dot of Fortitude or Protean
  - Two additional dots of migrating Herd
  - Feeding Flaw: Prey Exclusion (locals)

## Grim Reaper
Grim Reapers target those near death in care facilities, preying on the vulnerable.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Intelligence + Awareness/Medicine to find potential victims

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Awareness (Death) or Larceny (Forgery)
  - One dot of Auspex or Oblivion
  - One dot of Allies or Influence related to healthcare
  - One dot of Humanity
  - Feeding Flaw: (•) Prey Exclusion (Healthy Mortals)

## Montero
Monteros utilize teams to drive victims towards them, employing strategies similar to traditional hunting.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Intelligence + Stealth to plan and execute hunts
  - Resolve + Stealth for patient waiting

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Leadership (Hunting Pack) or Stealth (Stakeout)
  - One dot of Dominate or Obfuscate
  - Two dots of Retainers
  - Loss of one dot of Humanity

## Pursuer
Pursuers meticulously stalk their victims, waiting for the perfect opportunity to strike.

- **Dice Pools**: 
  - Intelligence + Investigation for locating a victim
  - Stamina + Stealth for stalking

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Investigation (Profiling) or Stealth (Shadowing)
  - One dot of Animalism or Auspex
  - Merit: (•) Bloodhound
  - One dot of Contacts
  - Loss of one dot of Humanity

## Trapdoor
Trapdoors build their lairs, luring victims in and then preying on them.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Charisma + Stealth to attract victims
  - Dexterity + Stealth for feeding

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Persuasion (Marketing) or Stealth (Ambushes or Traps)
  - One dot of Protean or Obfuscate
  - One dot of Haven
  - One dot of either Retainers or Herd
  - Haven Flaw: (•) Creepy or (•) Haunted

## Tithe Collector
Tithe Collectors require sufficient power to receive tributes in the form of vessels from other Kindred.

- **Dice Pool**: 
  - Manipulation + Intimidation for securing tributes

- **Gains**:
  - One specialty in either Intimidation (Kindred) or Leadership (Kindred)
  - One dot of Dominate or Presence
  - Three dots of Domain or Status
  - Adversary Flaw (••)

---

## Conclusion
Each of these Predator options provides unique gameplay mechanics and narratives, enhancing the deep storytelling and immersive experiences in Vampire: The Masquerade.
